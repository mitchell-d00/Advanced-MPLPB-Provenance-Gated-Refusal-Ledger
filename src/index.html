Original protocol ↗
01
Recruit independently
At least six corpus owners across distinct workflows. Code authors do not write, label, adjudicate, or inspect final questions before results lock.
02
Freeze before execution
Register code, corpus hashes, questions, rubric, sampling, seeds, and thresholds. Keep development and final-test organizations separate.
03
Blind, label, adjudicate
Two independent reviewers cite exact evidence. A third adjudicates before predictions are exposed. Retain disagreement and exclusions.
04
Publish every outcome
Report false acceptance, precision, coverage, refusals, source pins and failures. Protect confidential corpora and retain an audit procedure.
